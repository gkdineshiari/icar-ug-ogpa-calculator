💡 Strategies to Improve Your OGPA
📌 Attendance First
One SA grade (shortage of attendance) gives zero GP — it can drop your GPA by 0.3–0.5 points instantly. Attend ≥75% without exception.
🧪 Practicals = Easy GP
Lab records, viva and practical files are easier to score high than theory. Consistent lab effort adds 0.4–0.6 GP per course reliably.
🎯 Target 3-Credit Courses
A 3-credit course impacts OGPA 3× more than a 1-credit course. Scoring 8.5+ in every 3-credit course is the fastest route to First Division.
📝 Win Quizzes Early
Four quizzes = 20% of theory marks. Acing all four before mid-term gives a head-start that cushions end-term pressure significantly.
🔁 Clear in First Attempt
Only the latest attempt grade counts. Repeating a failed course is recovery, not bonus. A first-attempt 6.5 beats a repeat 7.0 in OGPA impact.
🌱 Student READY = 20 Credits
Semester VIII carries 20 credits with full GP potential. Excellent RAWE report + active industry engagement = 8.5+ for the entire semester.
📚 First Two Years Are Gold
A 7.0 GPA in Sem I permanently lifts your cumulative floor. Low early GPAs cannot be fully recovered even with 9.0s later — start strong.
🤝 Know the Marking Pattern
Request model answer scripts after each exam. Understanding exactly what markers reward can reliably add 5–10 marks per paper.
